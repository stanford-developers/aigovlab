About the lab

The AI Governance Lab focuses on creating the institutional framework for the AI transition. We study how law, policy, and institutions can adapt to rapidly advancing artificial intelligence, and what changes will be necessary in the early years before we enter the post-AGI society.

The lab's work is inherently interdisciplinary, drawing on political science, philosophy, law, computer science, and policy. Because AI shapes every field, governing it well requires the perspectives of each of these disciplines.

Training is central to the lab. Students, predoctoral and postdoctoral scholars lead their own projects with close mentorship, supported by research funding, weekly lab meetings, and collaboration across the team.
