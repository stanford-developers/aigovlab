Dominic Zappia is a Research Associate with the Technology Ethics & Policy
Rising Scholars Program at the McCoy Family Center for Ethics in Society,
working with Professor Rob Reich. A Midwest native, Dominic graduated from the
University of Wisconsin–Madison with a B.A. in Economics, Political Science,
and Philosophy. His senior honors thesis combined epistemology and democratic
theory with data analysis to explore how digital design can promote
democratic values, and he served as Chair of UW–Madison's Student Government.

Dominic is interested in how emerging technologies such as AI interact with
democratic values, and how responsible governance can align the interests of
industry, government, and the public.
