Roberta Fischli is a political scientist, a Postdoctoral Researcher in the
Department of Political Science, and a member of the AI Governance Lab.
Roberta's research asks how we can protect human liberty in an age of advanced
machine intelligence, and what future-proof institutions require.

Previously, Roberta was an AGI Fellow at the Johns Hopkins School of Government
and Policy and a Visiting Faculty Researcher at Google, leading a project on AI
agents and value alignment, with earlier research appointments at UC Berkeley,
Georgetown Law, and the University of St. Gallen. Roberta holds a PhD (summa
cum laude) in Political Economy and International Affairs from St. Gallen,
with a dissertation on the changing meaning of personal freedom in the
algorithmic age. The work has appeared in *Perspectives on Politics*, *Minds
and Machines*, the *European Journal of Political Theory*, and *Philosophy
Now*. Before becoming a researcher, Roberta worked as a freelance journalist.
