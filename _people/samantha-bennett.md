Samantha Augusta Bennett is a PhD Candidate at Stanford University, where she
examines risk, safety, and ethical issues related to emerging technologies,
especially artificial intelligence (AI). Her current research focuses on
decision theory, specifically how safety decisions are made under uncertainty.
She is also developing work on the precautionary principle and how it relates
to recent advances in AI agents. She is an Affiliate of the Stanford
Existential Risks Initiative (SERI), where she led the 2026 Summer Fellowship
Program. She was previously a Fellow at the Stanford Institute for
Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) in 2025 – 2026 and at the McCoy
Family Center for Ethics in Society in 2024 – 2025. Recently, she presented
work at the Bovay Engineering and Applied Ethics Workshop on AI Value
Alignment, KU Leuven’s International Conference on Large-Scale AI Risks, and
the Canadian Association for the Philosophy of Artificial Intelligence (CAPAI)
Symposium on Artificial Intelligence. Before coming to Stanford, she worked
across tech, finance, and law, getting her hands dirty with everything from
assessing AI companies to helping individuals pursue legal action for Clean Air
Act violations, exposures to dangerous pesticides, and data breaches. Samantha
holds an MA in Philosophy (with a concentration in Mathematics) summa cum laude
from the University of St Andrews and a law conversion degree.
