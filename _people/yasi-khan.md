Yasi Khan is an IRiSS Predoctoral Scholar in the AI Governance Lab and an
affiliate of Stanford HAI, studying how democratic societies can shape AI
policy that upholds their political, social, and economic futures. Yasi's
research focuses on accelerationism in Silicon Valley and its effect on the
race for AGI, part of a broader project on the origins of techno-ideologies and
their relationship to modern political movements.

Yasi holds an MSc with Distinction in Social Science of the Internet from the
University of Oxford, earned at Balliol College as a Clarendon Scholar, and a
B.S. with Honors and Distinction in Symbolic Systems from Stanford, with a
minor in Poetry. Before joining the lab, Yasi was a business analyst at
McKinsey & Company, helping establish its AI Trust practice.
