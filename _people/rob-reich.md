Rob Reich is Co-Director of the AI Governance Lab and a professor of political
science at Stanford, with courtesy appointments in Philosophy, Law, and
Education. Rob teaches and writes about the governance of frontier science and
technology, and has written previously about philanthropy in democratic
societies and the purposes of public education.

Rob is associate director of the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI and
faculty director of the Ethics, Society, and Technology Initiatives at the
McCoy Family Center for Ethics in Society. Rob was an early advisor to Giving
Tuesday and served as its founding board chair.
