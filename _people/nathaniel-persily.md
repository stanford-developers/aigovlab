Nate Persily is the James B. McClatchy Professor of Law at Stanford Law School
and Co-Director of the Stanford Law AI Initiative, with appointments in
Political Science, Communication, and the Freeman Spogli Institute for
International Studies. His work focuses on the law of democracy and the
governance of technology. He is a coauthor of the casebook *The Law of
Democracy*, has served as a special master or court-appointed expert drawing
districting plans for seven states, and was Senior Research Director for the
Presidential Commission on Election Administration.

His current work, recognized with Guggenheim and Andrew Carnegie fellowships,
examines the governance of technology and its impact on democracy. He coedited
*Social Media and Democracy* and *Artificial Intelligence, Politics, and
Political Science* with Joshua Tucker, and *The Digitalist Papers* with Erik
Brynjolfsson, Alex Pentland, and Condoleezza Rice. He cochairs the American
Political Science Association's Presidential Task Force on Artificial
Intelligence and is a member of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences. He
holds a B.A. and M.A. from Yale, a J.D. from Stanford, and a Ph.D. in political
science from UC Berkeley.
